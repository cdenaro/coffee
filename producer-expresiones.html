Expresiones

Expresiones is a family-run specialty coffee company in the Quindío region of Colombia, working across four farms with innovative processing methods rarely seen outside of competition coffees — osmotic dehydration, carbonic maceration, extended fermentations, and thermal action washes.

Eight distinctive lots make up the Expresiones collection, each one a study in variety and process.

A visit to the farms

A short video from the Expresiones team showing life on the farm and the processing work behind the collection.

Four farms, one philosophy

Expresiones coffees come from four partner farms in Quindío: Finca La Sirena in Calarcá, Finca La Macarena in Pijao, Finca La Linda in Buenavista, and Finca Buenos Aires in Génova. Each farm sits in Colombia's central coffee-growing highlands, between roughly 1,550 and 1,800 meters above sea level.

What sets the Expresiones collection apart is process. Rather than sticking to the classic washed protocol, the team experiments with techniques more commonly seen in competition lots — osmotic dehydration with fruit co-fermentations on the Bourbon Rojo, a 75% mucilage honey on the Bourbon Naranja, carbonic maceration on the Geisha, and naturals on the Java and Wush Wush. The result is a collection that showcases how dramatically processing shapes cup character.
