Expresiones Bourbon Rojo

Finca La Sirena · Calarcá, Quindío · Bourbon Rojo Variety · Osmotic Dehydration with Watermelon · 1,630 msnm

$26

Bourbon Rojo is a descendant of the Bourbon line that spread from Réunion Island to producing regions around the world. It's known for a deeply sweet, creamy cup with bright acidity and notes of vanilla, chocolate, caramel, and red fruit. In this edition, producer Juan David Puerta at Finca La Sirena uses an osmotic dehydration process with watermelon — a technique that draws out the coffee's natural fruit character through a co-fermentation logic. The result is a juicy, expressive, and deeply differentiated cup. It's one of our bestsellers.

Tasting Notes

Tropical fruits, cinnamon, flowers, and chocolate. Juicy citric acidity to blueberry. Silky mouthfeel with a lingering finish of hazelnut cream and watermelon.

Brew Guide

Pour-over recommended at 192°F. Weigh your beans. Take your time.

Producer: Juan David Puerta. Finca La Sirena, Calarcá, Quindío. Direct and fair trade. Zero pesticides. Regenerative farming.
