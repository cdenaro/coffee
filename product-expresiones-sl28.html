SL-28 is the legendary Kenyan variety prized the world over, grown here at Finca Bellavista in Quimbaya, Quindío and processed naturally. Ripe pineapple, plums, and flowers meet a creamy, caramelized coconut texture with a residual hint of cinnamon, cardamom, and anise.
